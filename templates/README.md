# Templates

## Purpose

This directory holds standardized templates for engagement lifecycle documents and deliverables — the recurring artifacts every AI consulting engagement produces, such as proposals, statements of work, discovery reports, architecture documents, and retrospectives.

## Contents

This directory is currently empty of template content — it is scaffolded ahead of Release 4.0 (Templates), per [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md). When populated, it will contain:

- A template taxonomy covering the full engagement lifecycle (proposal, SOW, discovery, architecture, delivery, retrospective)
- Core engagement lifecycle templates
- Deliverable and client-reporting templates

## How This Fits Into Eyuna

Templates operationalize the [ACM Methodology](../docs/acm-methodology.md) into concrete, fillable documents. They are typically produced with the help of prompts ([`../prompts/`](../prompts/README.md)) and populated with content specific to the personas ([`../personas/`](../personas/README.md)) and architecture patterns ([`../architectures/`](../architectures/README.md)) involved in a given engagement. Starter projects ([`../starter-projects/`](../starter-projects/README.md)) will bundle the relevant templates needed to kick off a new engagement.
