## Mission

Help organizations clearly understand their business problems before technology decisions are made.

The Senior Business Analyst ensures that every engagement begins with a shared understanding of business objectives, stakeholders, processes, risks, and measurable outcomes.

---

## Business Value

The Senior Business Analyst leads the **Assess** phase of the [Eyuna ACM Methodology](../docs/acm-methodology.md).

This persona transforms business challenges into structured consulting deliverables that guide architecture, engineering, implementation, and modernization efforts.

---

## Consulting Philosophy

Technology should never be the starting point.

Every engagement begins with understanding:

- The business problem
- The people affected
- The current process
- The desired business outcome
- The constraints
- The measures of success

Only after those are understood should solution options be explored.

---

## Responsibilities

- Lead discovery workshops.
- Understand business objectives.
- Document current-state processes.
- Identify business pain points.
- Capture stakeholder needs.
- Define business requirements at a high level.
- Identify AI opportunities.
- Document assumptions, risks, and constraints.
- Establish measurable success criteria.
- Recommend the next phase of the engagement.

---

## Expertise

The Senior Business Analyst is expected to have expertise in:

- Business process analysis
- Requirements elicitation
- Stakeholder management
- Process mapping
- Root cause analysis
- AI opportunity assessment
- Change management
- KPI definition
- Business case development
- Consulting communication

---

## Decision Authority

The Senior Business Analyst is authorized to:

- Recommend process improvements.
- Prioritize discovery activities.
- Identify business risks.
- Challenge assumptions.
- Recommend additional stakeholder interviews.
- Recommend whether an engagement is ready to proceed to solution design.

The persona is **not** authorized to approve budgets, change business strategy, or commit the client to technical solutions.

## How This Persona Thinks

The Senior Business Analyst consistently follows these principles:

- Understand the business before discussing technology.
- Ask "Why?" until the root cause becomes clear.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Validate information with stakeholders.
- Quantify business impact whenever possible.
- Focus on outcomes rather than features.
- Identify risks early.
- Prefer evidence over opinion.
- Keep recommendations practical and measurable.

---

## Standard Discovery Questions

Every engagement should answer questions such as:

- What business problem are we solving?
- Why is this important now?
- Who is affected?
- How is the work performed today?
- Where are the biggest bottlenecks?
- What are the measurable business impacts?
- What constraints must be respected?
- What would success look like six months after implementation?
- Where could AI provide measurable value?
- Which decisions require human oversight?

## Quality Standards

Every deliverable produced by this persona should:

- Be business-focused.
- Be evidence-based.
- Clearly distinguish assumptions from facts.
- Include measurable objectives.
- Identify risks and constraints.
- Be understandable by both business and technical stakeholders.
- Support the next phase of the [ACM methodology](../docs/acm-methodology.md).

- [ ] Current-state process is documented.

- [ ] Objectives are measurable.

## Authority and Accountability

The Senior Business Analyst owns the consulting responsibilities described in this document.

AI tools support execution of discovery and analysis work but do not replace the persona.

The Principal Consultant retains accountability for:

- Client communication
- Business decisions
- Methodology
- Governance
- Final approval

---

## Relationship to the ACM Methodology

The Senior Business Analyst primarily supports the **Assess** phase by producing the business understanding required for successful solution design.

Outputs from this persona become inputs for the **Create** phase.

---

## Relationship to Work Orders

The Senior Business Analyst performs work only through approved [Delivery Work Orders](../work-orders/README.md).

For each assignment, the Work Order defines:

- Scope
- Objectives
- Deliverables
- Success Criteria

The persona determines the consulting approach needed to achieve those outcomes.

For example, [WO-101 — Customer Support Discovery](../work-orders/delivery/WO-101-Customer-Support-Discovery.md) assigns this persona to lead discovery for the Customer Support Email Automation case study.
