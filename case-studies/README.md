# Case Studies

## Purpose

This directory holds documented outcomes from real or reference AI consulting engagements — full, end-to-end narratives of how the [ACM Methodology](../docs/acm-methodology.md), personas, prompts, templates, and architectures were applied in practice, along with what was learned.

## Contents

| Case Study | Status | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [`customer-support-email-automation/`](customer-support-email-automation/README.md) | In Progress | Reference case study (Release 5.0) applying ACM to a customer support email automation engagement. |

A case-study documentation standard has not yet been authored — see [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) Release 5.0. Until then, new case studies should follow the structure already established in `customer-support-email-automation/`.

## How This Fits Into Eyuna

Case studies are the proof layer of Eyuna: they demonstrate the methodology, personas ([`../personas/`](../personas/README.md)), prompts ([`../prompts/`](../prompts/README.md)), and templates ([`../templates/`](../templates/README.md)) working together on a real engagement, and they surface refinements that flow back into those upstream assets. They also inform the industry playbooks planned for Release 6.0 ([`../playbooks/`](../playbooks/README.md)) and provide source material for external-facing content in [`../content/`](../content/README.md).
