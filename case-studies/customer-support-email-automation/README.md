## Executive Summary

This case study demonstrates how the Eyuna consulting methodology can be used to transform a traditional customer support operation using modern AI technologies.

The objective is to reduce manual effort, improve response times, increase customer satisfaction, and enable support teams to focus on high-value interactions while maintaining human oversight for sensitive communications.

This engagement follows the complete Eyuna consulting lifecycle, from business discovery through production-ready implementation.

---

## Business Scenario

A mid-sized SaaS company receives approximately **8,000 customer support emails every day**.

The support team manually reviews every email to:

- Identify the customer's issue
- Determine its priority
- Route it to the correct department
- Draft a response
- Escalate critical issues

As the company has grown, this manual process has become increasingly difficult to scale.

Customers are experiencing slower response times, support agents spend significant time on repetitive work, and management lacks visibility into support trends and operational efficiency.

The company wants to leverage AI to improve the customer experience while preserving quality, compliance, and human oversight.

---

## Business Challenges

Current challenges include:

- Manual email triage
- Slow first-response times
- Inconsistent ticket prioritization
- High operational costs
- Repetitive work for support agents
- Limited reporting and operational insights
- Difficulty scaling during demand spikes

---

## Business Objectives

The engagement aims to:

- Reduce first-response time by at least 70%
- Automatically classify incoming support emails
- Detect urgent and high-risk customer requests
- Generate AI-assisted response drafts
- Route requests to the correct support team
- Maintain human approval for sensitive responses
- Improve customer satisfaction (CSAT)
- Reduce operational costs

---

## Success Metrics

The success of the engagement will be measured using:

- First Response Time (FRT)
- Average Resolution Time (ART)
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Percentage of Emails Automated
- Agent Productivity
- Routing Accuracy
- Escalation Accuracy
- Cost per Support Ticket

---

## Constraints

The solution must:

- Protect personally identifiable information (PII)
- Support human review for sensitive communications
- Integrate with existing customer support tools
- Minimize disruption to current support operations
- Provide monitoring, auditability, and governance
- Be designed for enterprise-scale deployment
