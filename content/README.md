# Content

## Purpose

This directory holds external-facing content that promotes and explains Eyuna and the consulting work it enables — as distinct from the internal methodology, personas, and prompts used to actually deliver engagements.

## Contents

| Subdirectory | Description |
|---|---|
| [`website/`](website/README.md) | Content for Eyuna's public website (pages, articles) |
| [`linkedin/`](linkedin/README.md) | Content drafted for LinkedIn and similar professional social channels |

Both subdirectories are currently placeholders — no external-facing content has been authored yet (see [PROJECT_STATUS.md](../PROJECT_STATUS.md)).

## How This Fits Into Eyuna

Content is the outward-facing layer of Eyuna. It draws on the internal methodology ([`../docs/`](../docs/README.md)), case studies ([`../case-studies/`](../case-studies/README.md)), and playbooks ([`../playbooks/`](../playbooks/README.md)) as source material for articles and posts, but it does not itself define methodology — it communicates it to an external audience.
