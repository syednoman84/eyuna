# ACM Methodology

> **Status:** Placeholder — not yet authored. Tracked under Release 1.0 in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Purpose of This Document

ACM (AI Consulting Methodology) is Eyuna's core engagement methodology. This document will define:

- The phases every AI consulting engagement moves through, from initial discovery to delivery and handover
- The inputs, activities, and outputs expected at each phase
- How personas ([`../personas/`](../personas/README.md)), prompts ([`../prompts/`](../prompts/README.md)), templates ([`../templates/`](../templates/README.md)), and architectures ([`../architectures/`](../architectures/README.md)) plug into each phase
- How engagement quality and outcomes are evaluated against the methodology

## Why It Matters

ACM is what makes Eyuna a *methodology*, not just a content library. Every persona, prompt, template, and playbook in this repository is expected to map back to a phase or activity defined in ACM. The [Customer Support Case Study](../case-studies/README.md) planned for Release 5.0 will be the first full, end-to-end demonstration of ACM applied to a real engagement type.
