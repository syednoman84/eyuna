# Consulting Principles

> **Status:** Placeholder — not yet authored. Tracked under Release 1.0 in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Purpose of This Document

This document will define the principles that guide *how* consulting work is approached and delivered within Eyuna — independent of any specific methodology phase, industry, or engagement type. It will cover things such as:

- How to engage with client ambiguity and evolving requirements
- Standards of transparency, evidence, and rigor expected in AI recommendations
- How to balance reusable framework guidance with engagement-specific judgment
- Ethical and responsible-AI considerations that apply across all engagements

## Why It Matters

Where the [ACM Methodology](acm-methodology.md) defines the *process* of an engagement, Consulting Principles define the *judgment* consultants apply within that process. Personas ([`../personas/`](../personas/README.md)) and prompts ([`../prompts/`](../prompts/README.md)) should reflect these principles consistently, regardless of which industry playbook ([`../playbooks/`](../playbooks/README.md)) they are used within.
