# Eyuna Constitution

> **Status:** Placeholder — not yet authored. Tracked under Release 1.0 in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Purpose of This Document

The Constitution is Eyuna's foundational charter. It will define:

- Why Eyuna exists and what problem it solves
- The scope and boundaries of the framework (what Eyuna is, and explicitly what it is not)
- The core commitments Eyuna makes to anyone who adopts it (consultants, teams, partners, clients)
- The non-negotiable standards that all other Eyuna content — methodology, personas, prompts, templates, playbooks — must be consistent with

## Why It Matters

Every other document in Eyuna derives its authority from this one. The [ACM Methodology](acm-methodology.md) describes *how* engagements are run; the [Consulting Principles](consulting-principles.md) describe *how* consultants should behave; the Constitution describes *why* any of it exists and what it is ultimately in service of.
