# Governance

> **Status:** Placeholder — not yet authored. Tracked under Release 1.0 in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Purpose of This Document

This document will define how Eyuna itself is governed as a project. It will cover:

- Decision-making authority and process for accepting content and structural changes
- Ownership and stewardship responsibilities for each top-level directory
- How the Architecture Decision Record process in [`DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md) is used and enforced
- How disputes or disagreements about methodology, structure, or content are resolved
- How the roadmap in [`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) is maintained and re-prioritized over time

## Why It Matters

Eyuna is designed to scale over many years and, potentially, many contributors. Without clear governance, a framework like this drifts — inconsistent content gets added, structure erodes, and the methodology loses coherence. This document is what keeps Eyuna's growth disciplined and its quality bar consistent, complementing the day-to-day process defined in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
